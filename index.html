How Seekive Works

Three simple steps to unlock a world of personalized discoveries

1

Set Your Vibe

Choose your category. Explore freely, or refine your search with optional filters for time, budget, distance, mood and more. Tell us what you're seeking today.

2

Spin the Compass

Watch as our intelligent compass spins and analyzes thousands of possibilities, considering weather, time of day, and your personal preferences to find the perfect match.

3

Discover & Experience

Get detailed information about your discovery, save it to your journal, share with your community, or spin again for another adventure. Every experience is a story waiting to be lived.
